Our 4-Step Turnkey Process

From initial design and engineering to live show execution.

01

3D Concept & Planning

Designing spatial layouts, stage blueprints, or booth 3D renders according to your vision.

02

CAD Specs & Audio Rigs

Drafting technical shop drawings, selecting high-grade materials, and audio-visual setups.

03

Precision Fabrication

Building custom structures, testing DJ sound equipment, and pre-assembling components.

04

Live Show Operation & Execution

Managing live events, operating in-house DJ & performance acts, direct sound engineering, and full show control from start to finish.
